## Our policy

Because Wovenfate delivers digital content instantly — a title unlocks the moment your payment is confirmed — **we're unable to offer refunds once a title or bundle has been unlocked.**

This is standard practice for instant-access digital content, and reflects that under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, the standard 14-day "cooling off" right to cancel doesn't apply once you've agreed to immediate digital delivery and that delivery has begun — which happens automatically the moment your purchase completes.

By completing a purchase, you acknowledge and agree that:
- You are requesting immediate access to the digital content
- You understand that this means you give up the standard 14-day cancellation right that applies to physical goods

## Exceptions

We will investigate and, where appropriate, issue a refund for:
- **Duplicate charges** — if you were accidentally charged twice for the same title
- **Payment processing errors** — if a technical fault meant you were charged but never received access
- **Unauthorised transactions** — if you believe a purchase was made without your permission

## How to request a refund under these exceptions

Email **support@wovenfate.app** with:
- The email address or account used for the purchase
- The title(s) involved
- A brief description of the issue

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights under UK consumer law, including your right to a refund or repair for digital content that is faulty or not as described.
