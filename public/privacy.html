Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Wovenfate is operated by wovenfate.app. You can contact us at support@wovenfate.app.

2. What data we collect

If you just read for free, as a guest:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous account identifier (so your reading progress can be saved), stored on your device
  • Which chapter you've reached in each title, and the choices you've made, so your story can resume correctly

If you choose to add an email to your account (to protect a purchase across devices, or save progress permanently):

  • Your email address
  • The same reading progress and purchase data, now linked to that email instead of only the device

If you choose to get emails about new books (optional, off unless you tick the box):

  • Your email address and a record of your choice: the wording you agreed to, when you opted in, and when you opted out if you do

How the site is used:

  • Which chapters are opened, which choices are made and which endings are reached, linked to your anonymous or account identifier, so we can see where stories work and where readers stop
  • Where a visit came from: the website that linked to us (just its address, e.g. tiktok.com) or a campaign tag in the link you followed

If you make a purchase:

  • A record that you own a specific title (or the full library) — we do not see or store your card number, expiry date, or CVC. That's handled entirely by Stripe; see their privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Narration and voice preferences (which voice you've chosen, auto-read settings) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device — we never see or store these on our servers.

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

3. How we use this data

  • To let you read, resume, and unlock stories
  • To protect your purchases from being lost if you clear your browser or switch devices
  • To improve the stories and the site, using the reading and visit information above
  • To email you when new books come out, only if you've opted in. We send no more than two of these a month. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time: untick the box in your account (the person icon at the top of the site), use the unsubscribe link in any of these emails, or email support@wovenfate.app. Opting out doesn't affect your account, progress or purchases.
  • To respond if you contact us for support
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations

4. Who we share data with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run Wovenfate, each of whom processes data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting of your account and progress data) — data is hosted in the EU (eu-west-1)
  • Stripe (payment processing) — handles your card details directly; we never see them
  • Vercel (web app hosting)
  • Resend (email delivery) — sends your sign-in links and, if you've opted in, our emails about new books

We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't sell it.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account, progress, and purchase data for as long as your account exists, so purchases remain accessible. If you ask us to delete your account (see below), we'll delete your personal data, though we may retain purchase records as required for tax/accounting purposes. If you opt out of emails about new books, we stop sending them straight away but keep the record of when you opted in and out, so we can show we only emailed you with your permission.

6. Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU, you have rights under GDPR, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, email support@wovenfate.app. We'll respond within the timeframes required by law.

7. Children's privacy

Wovenfate is intended for adults (18+) due to mature story themes. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the Service is not directed at them.

8. International transfers

Our data is primarily hosted in the EU. If any data is transferred outside the UK/EEA (for example, via a service provider's infrastructure), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as required by UK GDPR.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email support@wovenfate.app. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
